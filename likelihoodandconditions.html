📌 Grammar Notes: Likelihood & Consequences

According to our class notes:

  • The most probable (#0): IF PRESENT - THEN PRESENT. Something that always happens the same way.
    "If you heat water in a stove, the water definitely boils."
  • The 75% probability (#1): IF PRESENT - THEN FUTURE. We use it to anticipate consequences.
    "If my dog eats a bar of chocolate, he'll probably get very sick."
  • The 50/50 probability (#2): IF SUBJUNCTIVE PRESENT (SIMPLE PAST) - THEN IMAGINARY CONSEQUENCE (WOULD).
    "If I won the lottery, I would travel around the world."

💡 TIP: Adverbs of likelihood usually come before the main verb. She will probably call you. (NOT: She will call you probably.)
