📌 Grammar Notes

  • Likelihood Adverbs: Describe probability (certainly, definitely, probably, likely, possibly, unlikely, not really, not at all). They generally go before the main verb (e.g., She will probably call.).
  • Zero Conditional: Facts/General truths (If + Present, Present).
  • First Conditional: Real/Possible future situations (If + Present, will + Base Verb).
  • Second Conditional: Imaginary/Unreal present or future situations (If + Past Simple, would + Base Verb).

  8. 8. I (not / do) that if I were you.
