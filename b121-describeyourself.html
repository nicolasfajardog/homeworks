Giving a short description of myself in an interview 🗣️

Vocabulary: Personality Traits  |  Language Focus: Adjective & Adverb Intensifiers

🧩 Vocabulary

reserved sociable outgoing shy easygoing strict friendly assertive talkative quiet fun-loving serious optimistic pessimistic extroverted introverted

📈 Language Focus

Intensifiers are placed before adjectives to modify their strength.

  • Strong: very, so, really
  • Moderate: pretty, quite, rather, fairly

Example: "I am quite shy, but very friendly."

💬 Communication

Useful structures for interviews:

  • "I’m a [adjective] person..."
  • "He/She tends to be..."
  • "One thing you should know about them is that they are..."
  • "If I had to describe them in one or two words, that would be..."
  • "What stands out most about him is that he is..."
  • "I consider myself to be..."
