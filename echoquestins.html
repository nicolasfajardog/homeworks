📌 Grammar Notes: Clarifying Information

1. Asking for Repetitions / Rectifications:

  • Casual: "Can you speak more slowly, please?"
  • Formal: "Would you repeat that, please? I didn't catch it." or "Could you clarify what you mean by that?"

2. Tag Questions (To Confirm Info):

Use the opposite auxiliary verb of the main sentence. If the sentence is positive, the tag is negative, and vice versa.

  • "The logistics team has shipped the goods, hasn't they?"
  • "We rarely use air freight, do we?" (Words like rarely, never, hardly make the sentence negative!)
  • Exceptions: "I am" → "aren't I?", "Let's" → "shall we?"

3. Echo Questions (To show surprise or ask for specific repetition):

Replace the surprising or missing information with a WH-word, keeping the statement structure and using rising intonation.

  • Vendor: "We raised the unit price to $500."
  • Buyer: "You raised the unit price to HOW MUCH?!"
  • Vendor: "I sent the invoice to Batman."
  • Buyer: "You sent the invoice to WHO?!"
