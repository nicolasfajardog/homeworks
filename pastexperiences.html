Describing Past Experiences

Welcome to your homework! This assignment focuses on describing professional milestones and past experiences, drawing inspiration from our class notes in image_956a7e.png.

Remember the rules:

  • Past Perfect (had + past participle): For a completed action that happened before another action in the past.
  • Past Perfect Progressive (had been + verb-ing): For an action that was in progress up to a specific point in the past, focusing on duration.
