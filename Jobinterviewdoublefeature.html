Grammar Professional Storytelling

📌 Grammar Notes

  • Past Responsibilities: Use phrases like "I was responsible for..." or "I was in charge of..." followed by a verb ending in -ing.
  • Telling a Story (Mistakes & Lessons): Set the context using the Past Progressive (was/were + -ing). Describe the specific event using the Past Simple. Structure your story with connection words like first, then, after that, once, and finally.
  • Describing Experience: Use the Present Perfect (have/has + participle) for past situations that continue in the present. Use the Present Perfect Progressive (have/has + been + -ing) for situations happening right now.
  • Time Markers: Use since, for, before, recently, and already to provide precise details about your timeline.
