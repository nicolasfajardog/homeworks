Describing Negative Attributes Politely

Softening Expressions · Personality Adjectives · Animal Similes

📋 Vocabulary: Negative Attributes

We all have flaws! Here are common adjectives used to describe negative personality traits.

Bossy: Always telling people what to do.
Stubborn: Refusing to change your mind or opinion.
Irresponsible: Not showing a proper sense of responsibility.
Inconsiderate: Thoughtlessly causing hurt or inconvenience to others.
Selfish: Lacking consideration for others; focused on oneself.
Short-tempered: Getting angry quickly and easily.
Unhelpful: Not willing to help someone.

📈 Language Focus: Softening Expressions

In English, it is often considered rude to use direct negative adjectives (e.g., "He is selfish"). Instead, we use softening expressions to make criticism sound more polite.

  • not the + -est: "He's not the most generous person." / "She's not the cleanest roommate."
  • not as ... as: "She is not as organized as she could be."
  • could be more ...: "They could be more punctual."
  • not too ...: "He's not too considerate."
Harsh: "My coworker is very lazy."
Softened: "My coworker could be more hardworking."

💬 Communication: Animal Similes

Native speakers often use similes (comparisons) with animals to describe personality traits in a colorful, exaggerated way.
