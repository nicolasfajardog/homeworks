📌 Grammar Notes: Structuring Past Timelines

  • Past Perfect (had + past participle): For a completed action that happened before another action in the past.
    "When I bought my first car, I had never had my own car."
  • Past Perfect Progressive (had been + verb-ing): For an action that was in progress up to a specific point in the past, focusing heavily on the duration.
    "In August 2021, I had been working there for 8 months."
